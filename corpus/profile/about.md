# About Sonny

From the About section of Sonny's portfolio website.

## What I do

I build AI systems that solve real problems and give people their time back.

I've always been fascinated by how things work. From repairing electronics and building
automation projects to developing AI-powered applications, I've spent years learning how
systems fit together and how they can be improved. Today, I combine that curiosity with
software engineering and AI to build practical solutions that create real-world impact.

My work focuses on building intelligent systems that automate repetitive tasks,
streamline business processes, and help people make better use of their time. Whether
it's RAG systems, AI chatbots, workflow automation, or custom software, I enjoy turning
complex challenges into simple, scalable solutions.

For me, technology is about creating freedom. The best solutions aren't just technically
impressive, they solve meaningful problems and continue delivering value long after
they're built. That's what motivates me to keep learning, building, and improving every
day.

I'm always interested in discussing new opportunities, collaborating on interesting
projects, or simply connecting with fellow developers.
